Maternal-Guard & Life-Link
AI-Driven Maternal Care & Emergency Donor Network
1. VISION AND SOCIAL CONTEXT
In rural healthcare sectors, thegolden houris often lost due to a lack of data-driven insights
and fragmented communication. Maternal-Guard & Life-Link aims to bridge this gap.
By utilizing theMaternal Health Risk Dataset, this software transitions from reactive
to proactive care. The secondary goal is to create a digital bridge between academic institutes
and hospitals, ensuring that healthy, eligible donors are just a click away when postpartum
hemorrhage occurs.
2. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & SYSTEM DESIGN
The solution should be designed as a modular web application consisting of two primary
components:
Component A: The ML Risk Predictor
•Model Selection:Explore classifiers likeRandom Forest, XGBoost, or SVMto han-
dle the non-linear relationships in maternal vitals.
•Explainability (XAI):The model must output feature importance. If a patient’s
systolic BP is the primary risk driver, the UI must highlight this clearly for the doctor.
•Preprocessing:Handling outliers in heart rate and age within the Kaggle dataset is
essential for model stability.
Component B: The Life-Link Donor Network
•Database Schema:Use SQL or Firebase to manage:
1.User Profiles:Name, blood group, age, and location.
2.Medical Logs:Hemoglobin levels, chronic conditions, and timestamps.
•The Matching Algorithm:Filter by compatibility (e.g., O-negative universal donors)
and health scores (Hemoglobin>12.5 g/dL).
3. DETAILED MVP FEATURE SET
Phase 1: Diagnostic Input (The Health Worker Interface)
•Input Form:Mobile-responsive entry for Age, Systolic BP, Diastolic BP, Blood Glu-
cose, Body Temp, and Heart Rate.
•Real-time Validation:Immediate feedback if values are entered outside of human
physiological limits.

4. SECURITY AND ETHICAL CONSIDERATIONS
•Data Anonymization:High encryption for donor medical histories.
•Consent Management:A ”Donor Status” toggle for students to mark themselves
as ”Unavailable” (e.g., during exams) without deleting their profile.
•Bias Mitigation:Ensure the model does not unfairly penalize specific age groups
without medical justification.
